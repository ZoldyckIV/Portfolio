How I work

One person on the whole path of a click.

Most teams split this between a media buyer, a designer and a developer. I cover the full route, so the ad, the tracking and the page are planned together.

step 1

Campaign & creative

Campaign setup on Meta, Instagram and TikTok. I prepare ad visuals and adapt them to each placement.

  • Meta Ads
  • Instagram
  • TikTok Ads
  • Photoshop
step 2

Tracking

Pixels installed and tested, events named, UTM links built, so every visit is traceable back to the ad that sent it.

  • Meta Pixel
  • TikTok Pixel
  • UTM
  • Events
step 3

Landing pages & stores

Fast pages that match the ad they came from. Hand-coded when it needs to be custom, WordPress or Shopify when the client needs to edit it.

  • HTML/CSS/JS
  • Tailwind
  • Shopify
  • WordPress
  • Elementor
step 4

Web apps & hosting

Full apps with accounts, carts and dashboards, plus the servers, domains and deploys behind them.

  • React
  • Next.js
  • Laravel
  • ASP.NET
  • DigitalOcean
